# Beijing Visitor Itinerary — One-Day Driving Tour

A closed-loop driving itinerary for our visiting professor: arriving at Beijing Capital International Airport (PEK) in the morning and departing on an evening flight the same day.

**Route:** Airport → Temple of Heaven → National Museum of China → Summer Palace → Airport

All coordinates, addresses, elevations, opening hours, ratings and driving distances/times below are as reported by Google Maps.

## Practical Notes

- **National Museum of China is closed on Mondays** — confirm the visit day is a Tuesday–Sunday.
- The museum's last entry is well before its 4:30 PM closing time; plan the visit accordingly.
- Airport departure buffer: keep at least 2–3 hours at the airport before the evening flight, especially during Beijing rush hour.
- All times reflect typical traffic-free driving estimates from Google Maps; actual times may vary with traffic.
